## For Patients

Dr. Todd Hollon is a neurosurgeon at Michigan Medicine. He cares for patients with brain tumors, including pituitary adenomas, meningiomas, gliomas, and all other tumor types. He treats more than 100 brain tumor patients every year. His research in the MLiNS Lab develops artificial intelligence that helps surgeons diagnose and treat brain tumors.

## How to request an appointment

- **New patients:** call **[866-909-4449](tel:+18669094449)** to request an appointment.
- **Referring providers:** call M-LINE at **[800-962-3555](tel:+18009623555)**, or see [Refer or transfer a patient](https://www.uofmhealth.org/health-providers/refer-or-transfer-patient){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.
- You can also request an appointment through Dr. Hollon's [Michigan Medicine profile]({{ site.uofm_health_profile }}){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

Please have your recent imaging (MRI or CT) and medical records available when you call. Our clinic team can explain how to send them.

## Learn about our research

Many of our patients ask about the research behind their care. These pages explain it in plain language:

- [Intelligent Histology](/research/intelligent-histology/) - fast imaging and AI that help diagnose brain tumors during surgery, including [FastGlioma](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08169-3){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, which helps surgeons find tumor left behind after removal.
- [AI-based Neuroimaging](/research/neuroimaging/) - AI that reads brain MRI and CT scans.
- [Patient Forecasting](/research/patient-forecasting/) - predicting outcomes to help patients and families make informed decisions.
